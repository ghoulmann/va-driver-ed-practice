The project was inspired by a Northern Virginia driver-education computer-based training course. That course is not a source: every question here was written fresh, and every answer was verified against the primary sources listed below.

  • Virginia Department of Education — Curriculum Guide for Behind-the-Wheel In-Car Instruction, Module 13, including Appendix C, the 2022 Virginia Standards of Learning for Driver Education, which supplies this app's topic taxonomy.
  • The Code of Virginia, the Virginia DMV, the Virginia Department of Transportation and the Division of Legislative Services, whose published material is cited question by question in the sources section.
